# Maintenance Manager — BAE Systems (Aiken, SC)

## Summary
Owns all aspects of manufacturing maintenance performance at BAE's Aiken site, leading maintenance crafts, vendors and contractors across multiple shifts.

## Responsibilities
- Lead, supervise and develop maintenance crafts, vendors and contractors; ensure training and qualifications.
- Build relationships with operations leadership and the workforce; support site customers.
- Drive performance with industry benchmarks and KPIs.
- Lead continuous improvement, root cause analysis and corrective action.
- Set preventive maintenance scope and frequency, find predictive maintenance opportunities, and plan and schedule work to reduce downtime.
- Set priorities, forecast and allocate resources for corrective maintenance and projects.
- Apply lean manufacturing and TPM principles.
- Use Oracle eAM CMMS to schedule, track and report maintenance work.
- Manage facility equipment and tooling spare parts inventories.
- Lead the site Business Continuity Program; act as Incident Commander for facilities events.
- On call for emergencies; ensure regulatory compliance.

## Required qualifications
- Mgr I text: 15 years in a similar managerial role, or equivalent experience (its "typical" line says bachelor's + 8 years, or equivalent).
- Strong written and oral leadership and communication skills.
- Microsoft Office.

## Preferred qualifications
- Bachelor's in engineering, business or construction management.
- Reliability-centered maintenance and predictive maintenance.
- Developing and managing budgets; cost reduction.
- Metalworking equipment: machining centers, waterjets, lasers, blast and paint booths, cranes.
- Manufacturing planning, scheduling and staffing.
- Construction, testing and commissioning, preventive and corrective maintenance.

## My fit / notes
- Lead a 5-person welder/fabricator and millwright crew; help lead the plant's 50 maintenance techs; cover for the engineering manager.
- $20M+ maintenance budget and $1M CI budget; 12% OEE gain and 30% maintenance cost reduction.
- PM plans and spare parts in SAP (posting uses Oracle eAM), FMEAs, vibration/thermography with contractors, LOTO, guarding, safety RCAs.
- Gaps: years in a formal manager title, Lean/TPM training, Oracle eAM, business continuity / incident command.
- Prior DOE Q clearance (lapsed) is worth mentioning for a defense employer.
