# Lead Engineer - Mechanical — Kimberly-Clark

## Summary
Mechanical lead at the Beech Island Mill supporting daily case-handling performance, improving reliability, and leading capital projects that improve safety, quality, OEE and cost in the mill's logistics system.

## Responsibilities
- Work safely; design equipment and processes that meet safety codes, policies and procedures.
- Protect the safety and well-being of operators, maintenance staff and others.
- Support daily asset performance against safety, quality, OEE and cost targets.
- Lead design, development and optimization of manufacturing equipment, providing functional leadership and creative problem-solving.
- Lead product/process improvement projects up to ~$10M in financial scope, from concept through commercialization.
- Solve complex technical problems to root cause, including leading group problem-solving sessions.
- Build depth in engineering principles, scientific analysis, problem-solving and project management.
- Help develop others in your area of expertise.

## Required qualifications
- B.S. in Mechanical Engineering (or equivalent) from an accredited institution.
- 2+ years of work experience.
- Proven record of meeting objectives and following ways of working.
- Able to lead maintenance and operations teams through influence and problem-solving.
- Flexible to support a 24/7 manufacturing environment.
- Willing to travel up to 15%.
