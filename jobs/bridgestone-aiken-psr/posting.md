# Mechanical Engineer — Bridgestone Americas (Aiken PSR)

## Summary
Mechanical engineer at the Aiken passenger tire (PSR) plant supporting design, development, installation, optimization and reliability of manufacturing equipment, working across production, maintenance, quality and engineering to improve safety, reliability, productivity and cost.

## Responsibilities
- Design and implement mechanical systems, equipment and manufacturing processes; support utilities and auxiliary systems.
- Troubleshoot equipment, perform root cause analysis and drive corrective actions.
- Lead continuous improvement, with emphasis on scrap and waste reduction.
- Manage capital and CI projects (typically $50K–$1M), from equipment modifications to major machine installs: scope, budget, schedule, procurement, vendor selection, installation, startup, validation and closeout.
- Build safety requirements, risk assessments, machine safeguarding and regulatory compliance into engineering work, including contractor oversight.
- Support reliability improvements and preventive/predictive maintenance.
- Provide technical support to internal customers and input on new or modified systems.

## Required qualifications
- **Associate:** B.S. in ME or related; internship/co-op or up to 2 years' experience; strong problem-solving and communication.
- **Professional:** B.S. in ME or related; 3–5 years of engineering experience, preferably manufacturing; project management and CI experience; works independently and leads cross-functional initiatives.
- **Senior:** B.S. in ME or related; 7+ years with growing technical and project leadership; record of capital projects, installations and reliability work; mentoring experience.

## Preferred qualifications
- High-volume manufacturing experience.
- Machine design and equipment modernization.
- CAD (AutoCAD, Inventor, SolidWorks or similar).
- Industrial automation, robotics, hydraulics, pneumatics, mechanical systems.
- SAP or similar for procurement and cost management.
- Lean Manufacturing, Six Sigma or other CI methods.
- Budget, schedule and resource management.

## Compensation & benefits
- Competitive pay and benefits covering physical, emotional and financial well-being (retirement plans, financial planning); onboarding, training and mentorship. No figures listed.
- Form I-9 required; needing sponsorship may disqualify.

## My fit / notes
- Internal move: I'm currently a Senior ME at Bridgestone's Aiken OTR plant (July 2022–present). ~4.8 years total experience lines up with the **Professional** band (3–5 yrs).
- Strong matches: capital project management, RCA, safety-code compliance, reliability, contractor direction, machine design, SAP, CAD.
- Gaps to address in the interview or confirm before adding to the resume: specific scrap/waste reduction results, Lean/Six Sigma training, hydraulics/pneumatics/robotics depth, procurement and vendor selection on my projects, mentoring.
